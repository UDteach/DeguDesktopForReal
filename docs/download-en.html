Not sure which Mac you have?

Open the Apple menu at the top left and choose About This Mac. Look for “Chip” or “Processor.” If you see Intel, choose Intel Mac. If you see M1 or another M-series chip, choose M1 or later Mac.

After downloading

On Mac, open the latest DMG and move the app to Applications. Replace the older app if v0.6.1 said it was damaged. If first launch is blocked, try opening the app once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. The Mac app has no Apple Developer ID signature or notarization. On Windows, run the EXE.
