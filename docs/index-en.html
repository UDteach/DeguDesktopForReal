HOW IT WORKS

A small surprise
on your screen.

01

Shows up now and then

It pops up from below, peeks in from the side, or hops onto the screen. The app picks a motion each time.

02

Your wallpaper stays

A transparent window sits over your desktop. Your files and mouse clicks remain accessible.

03

Choose coat colors

Pick one color, several, or all 16. Set appearance intervals, sizes for each coat, and which monitors to use from the menu.

Settings are in the paw menu

On Mac, click the paw in the menu bar at the top of the screen. On Windows, find it in the system tray at the bottom right. You can also choose coat colors, appearance intervals, size, and monitors there.

  • Degu displayChoose the monitor with the cursor, the primary monitor, a specific monitor, or all monitors. All monitors show the same visitors. If your selection is disconnected, degus appear on the primary monitor.
  • Display sizeChoose a default size and override it for each coat color. Clear overrides and use one size restores the default for all coats.
  • PomodoroDegus stay away during focus and appear when a break begins. Change the focus and break lengths in Mode settings.
  • Remaining timeTurn the small countdown at the top right of the primary monitor on or off from the Pomodoro menu.
  • Degu swarmTurn it on to see five degus at once. Turn it off to return to normal appearances.
